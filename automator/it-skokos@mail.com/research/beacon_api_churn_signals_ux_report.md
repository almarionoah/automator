## 2. Key Churn Signals Identified

### Signal A: The Silent Despair (Failing Integration Velocity)
- **Pattern**: When a developer encounters >4 consecutive `429 Too Many Requests` or cryptic `500` series errors during their initial 7-day sandbox phase, retention probability drops by 62%.
- **Emotional Reality**: Frustration shifts into resignation; the developer abandons the integration without filing a ticket.

### Signal B: The Dimming Pulse (Call Diversity Decay)
- **Pattern**: Over 30 days, endpoint breadth contracts from 6 distinct endpoints to a single periodic polling endpoint before stopping entirely.
- **Emotional Reality**: The developer has scoped down reliance on Beacon API, preparing for migration.

---

## 3. Empathetic UX Interventions

1. **Contextual Resiliency Toasts**: Transform raw JSON error responses into conversational diagnostic guidance in the dashboard.
2. **Proactive Grace Periods**: Trigger proactive developer outreach when quota reaches 90% without an active billing escalation path.
