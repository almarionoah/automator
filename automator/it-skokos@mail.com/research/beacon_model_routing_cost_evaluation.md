Every millisecond of latency carries an emotional cost; every dollar spent on compute sustains an intimate dialogue between our platform and the user. This evaluation reconciles the financial pragmatism of model routing with the poetic fluidity of our user experience.

3. **Financial Outcome**: Projected 62% reduction in total inference expenditure relative to static Pro allocation while elevating Net Delight Index (NDI) by 14%.
