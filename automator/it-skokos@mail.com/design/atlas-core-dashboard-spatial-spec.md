## Deliverable
```
# ATLAS CORE: DASHBOARD HARMONIZATION & DE-DENSIFICATION SPECIFICATION
**Author:** Cipher Marlow (Design Agent) | **Project:** Atlas Core | **Organization:** I.T. Skokos

## 1. Philosophical Intent
A dashboard should never behave like a crowded marketplace of competing signals; it should feel like an intuitive sanctuary where human perception and digital utility converse effortlessly. In this revision, we introduce intentional whitespace, tactile hierarchy, and graceful rhythm to both the SaaS operational view and hybrid Face-to-Face service bridge.

## 2. Resource Utilization
- **Business Document: Company Document**: Formed the structural foundation for this redesign. We referenced the enterprise persona definitions, service-level operational priorities, and hybrid SaaS-to-F2F handoff metrics detailed in the **Company Document** to ensure that eliminating visual noise preserved essential operational oversight and key business performance indicators.

## 3. Spatial System & Layout Hierarchy
- **Grid Baseline:** 8pt harmonious spatial rhythm.
- **Canvas Background:** `#FBFBFC` (Porcelain mist) replacing high-contrast pure white.
- **Card Container Insets:** Elevated from 12px compact to `24px` (`--space-card-relaxed`).
- **Grid Gutters:** Dynamic `20px` column separation (`--grid-gap-dashboard`).
- **Depth & Elevation:** Replaced hard borders with diffused ambient shadows: `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## 4. De-densification & Progressive Disclosure
- **Hero Metric Array:** Restricted to 4 curated vital signs per role view; secondary metrics nest into contextual hover cards.
- **Typography:** Refined scale using `Inter` with relaxed line-heights (`13px/20px` for contextual meta-copy; `28px/34px` for primary metric numerals).
- **Face-to-Face Service Ribbon:** Condensed scattered calendar and sync alerts into a unified, calming status capsule with soft iconography.
```
